Deck TVWhat it can do

Home screen

  • Cardsone press opens an app full screen; press again to switch back to it
  • Groupsfolders of apps like Live TV and Streaming, each with its own look
  • Your layouthide, group and rearrange cards, by mouse or controller
  • Badgesunread WhatsApp messages and what's playing, right on the card
  • Weatherthe town's weather; press it for the five-day forecast

Controller

  • EverywhereD-pad or stick to move, A to choose, B to go back
  • Yundoes the last watchlist change

Switching apps

  • Docknudge the mouse to the bottom edge: Home, open apps and the sound menu
  • Alt+TabDeck TV's own switcher; hold Alt and tap, or click an app
  • HomeSuper+H or the dock's TV icon, from anywhere
  • Desktoptuck everything away in one press; Alt+F4 closes Deck TV

Now playing & sound

  • Now playingwhatever's playing, with play/pause and skip, wherever it is
  • One at a timestarting something new pauses the last
  • Soundvolume, and play on the TV, the Deck, headphones or Bluetooth
  • Speakerspair a new Bluetooth speaker from the sofa

Watchlist

  • ListsLiam and Laura, Top 100 (the scratch poster), Kids, or your own
  • Films & showsposter, plot, running time; seasons and years for shows
  • Watch on Movyopens Movy and searches it for you
  • Sort & filterto watch or watched; by title, year or length
  • Safety netUndo after marking watched, removing or moving

Trailers

  • Right hereplays inside Deck TV; nothing can take you off to YouTube
  • Controlsscrub, volume, captions, fullscreen
  • Every trailerTrailer 1, 2, 3…; one that won't play gives way to the next

Phone remote

  • Scan & gothe QR code on the home screen; no app to install
  • Touchpad & keysmouse, keyboard, app switching and media keys
  • Searchtype into Movy, YouTube or Spotify's search from the phone

System

  • At a glanceWi-Fi, battery and free space in the bottom row
  • System panelstorage (SD card and USB too), Wi-Fi, battery, temperature
  • Powersleep, restart or power off, with a countdown to cancel
  • Screensavera drifting clock when left alone; never over an app
